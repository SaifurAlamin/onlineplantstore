Monstera Care Guide

Everything you need to help your Monstera thrive.

☀️ Light Requirements

Bright, indirect light is ideal. Avoid harsh direct afternoon sun.

💧 Watering

Water when the top few centimetres of soil feel dry. Ensure good drainage.

🐾 Toxicity Level

Monstera contains insoluble calcium oxalates and is toxic to cats and dogs if chewed. Keep it out of their reach.

🪴 Repotting

Consider repotting when roots outgrow the pot, usually every 1–2 years. Choose a pot with drainage holes.

🌡️ Temperature

Prefers warm indoor conditions and protection from cold drafts.

🌱 Soil

Use a well-draining, airy potting mix suitable for tropical aroids.
